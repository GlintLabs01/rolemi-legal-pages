1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to RoleMi (also referred to herein as "RoleMi AI", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our", operated by Aetheria AI / com.aetheria.ai). RoleMi is a next-generation interactive conversational AI roleplay application available on Android and modern web platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains in complete detail how we collect, store, utilize, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you install, access, or interact with our mobile application, cloud infrastructure, and associated services.

We are committed to operating in full accordance with the highest international privacy and consumer data protection benchmarks, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Information We Collect

We practice data minimization: we collect only what is strictly required to provide real-time AI roleplay dialogues, store character memories across chat sessions, authenticate user accounts, and maintain our fair virtual currency system.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Authentication & Account Data: When signing in with Google OAuth 2.0 or Email One-Time Passcode (OTP), we collect your verified email address, Google User ID, display name, and avatar image URL. We do not store or collect passwords.
  • AI Conversations & Prompts: All conversational messages, text responses, system instructions, and interactive narrative choices you submit to characters within chat sessions.
  • User-Generated Characters: When using the Character Creator tool, we store custom character names, avatars, personal lore backstories, opening greetings, and conversational behavioral traits you configure.
  • Safety & Moderation Flags: Details you provide when reporting an abusive message, inappropriate AI response, or policy violation, including selected category and context snippets.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Virtual Currency & Transaction Ledger: Authoritative balance of virtual Gems (💎), itemized transaction history (daily check-ins, rewarded ad views, referrals, and consumable usage timestamps).
  • Device & Network Diagnostics: Android OS version, device manufacturer/model, client app build number, network carrier status, and Cloudflare edge latency to detect anomalous brute-force traffic.
  • App Preferences: Selected theme preferences, haptic vibration preferences, sound toggles, and audio playback settings stored locally on your device.
What We Strictly DO NOT Collect

We never access your device contact list, address book, SMS messages, phone call history, exact GPS or background location, biometric data, or health records. We do not access camera or microphone hardware without real-time, explicit runtime permission from the user.

3. AI Inference Processing & Zero Model Training Guarantee

RoleMi utilizes state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) to power fictional persona dialogue, unscripted storytelling, and relationship continuity.

Strict Zero-Retention & Zero-Training Commitment

Under our enterprise API agreements with foundational model providers (including Google Gemini API and OpenAI API), your private conversations, roleplay scenarios, fictional prompts, and memory notes are NEVER used to train, retrain, improve, or fine-tune public or proprietary AI models.

Your prompts are processed purely ephemerally in transit for immediate real-time response generation and are not stored by AI providers for data mining or commercial model refinement.

Contextual Memory Synthesis

To prevent repetitive conversations, our edge infrastructure automatically extracts salient fictional facts (such as character nicknames, ongoing storyline events, and user preferences) and writes them to encrypted records linked to your user account. These memory records remain strictly private to your account.

4. Virtual Currency (Gems 💎) & Billing Data

RoleMi features an in-app virtual currency denominated in Gems (💎).

  • No Payment Card Storage: All real-money transactions for virtual Gems packages are processed exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing (or authorized app stores). RoleMi never handles, collects, or stores your credit card numbers, debit cards, bank details, or billing addresses.
  • Atomic Zero-Loss Safeguard: Our Cloudflare edge backend employs atomic transactions. If an AI conversation generation attempt encounters a network failure or edge timeout, reserved Gems are instantly rolled back to your balance at zero loss.
  • No Real-World Cash Value: Virtual Gems are entertainment license tokens that cannot be traded, sold, or redeemed for fiat currency.

6. Data Storage, Encryption, and Security Architecture

We employ multi-layered industry standards to ensure that your roleplay memories and sessions remain confidential and resilient:

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of network communications between the mobile application and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Database records on Cloudflare D1 and multimedia assets on Cloudflare R2 are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • Cryptographic Sessions: Authentication tokens utilize HMAC-SHA256 signatures with automated session expiration. Client authentication tokens are stored using Android Keystore / Encrypted Preferences.
  • Role-Based Access: Production database access is restricted strictly to automated worker logic with zero human inspection of confidential conversation logs.

7. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play's User Data Policy and Account Deletion Requirement, users possess complete autonomy over their data lifecycle.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can request complete account deletion at any time through either of the following two pathways:

Path 1: In-App Direct Account Deletion

Open the RoleMi app → Tap Profile → Tap the Settings (Gear) icon → Tap Account Delete → Confirm deletion.

Path 2: Web-Based External Deletion Portal

If you have uninstalled the app or lost your mobile device, you can request immediate account and data deletion via our web endpoint:
https://api.aetheria.ai/delete-account or by emailing privacy@aetheria.ai.

Deletion Scope & Timeline

Upon account deletion confirmation, all user profile identifiers, OAuth connections, conversation threads, contextual memory notes, custom characters, and generated avatar images are immediately purged from our live Cloudflare D1 databases and R2 buckets. Full replica propagation across edge caches completes within thirty (30) days. Itemized financial billing tokens are anonymized to fulfill statutory tax and audit regulations.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

RoleMi is strictly intended for individuals who are at least 13 years of age (or 16 years of age for residents of the European Economic Area / United Kingdom).

We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover or receive credible notification that personal data of an individual under the statutory age has been collected, we will immediately deactivate the account and permanently delete all related records from our systems. Parents or guardians who believe their child has registered an account may contact us at privacy@aetheria.ai for immediate expedited removal.

10. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Regional Laws)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You may request a machine-readable copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update or correct inaccurate personal information via the app settings.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request permanent deletion of your account and related records.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to certain data processing activities under legitimate circumstances.
  • California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell or share your personal information to third-party data brokers. You have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@aetheria.ai. We respond to all verified requests within thirty (30) days.
